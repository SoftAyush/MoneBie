Built from the supplied res/drawable/logo.xml, placed on a deep navy plate. This is what is in the project now.
Navy, not white. The logo's ribbon faces are white and pale cyan — on a light plate they vanish. Navy keeps every edge.
Inset 22dp a side. Leaves the artwork about 62dp wide, clearing the 66dp guaranteed circle with room under any launcher mask.
Mirroring turned off. The file arrived with autoMirrored="true", which flips a logo horizontally in right-to-left locales.

What a traced logo costs
656 paths, 492 KB. The file is a raster image auto-traced into hundreds of near-identical shades, not a drawn vector. It inflates and rasterises on every use; Android's own guidance is to keep a VectorDrawable far simpler than this.
It blurs below about 64dp. The bars and the orbit ribbon merge into a blue mass. The launcher grid is where an icon is seen most.
No themed-icon layer. Flattening 656 tinted paths to one silhouette gives a blob, so that layer is left out and Android 13+ falls back to the full-colour icon.
Redrawing this same design as a handful of clean paths would fix all three. The shape is good; it is the tracing that is expensive.
